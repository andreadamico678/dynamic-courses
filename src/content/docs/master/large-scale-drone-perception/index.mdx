## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Large-scale Drone Perception** (`T550060101`, **5 ECTS**, Semester 2) focuses on extracting metric geometry, motion, and environmental understanding from cameras mounted on flying platforms. Aerial perception brings unique headaches compared to stationary or ground cameras: wide baselines, altitude-induced scale changes, motion blur from vibrations, massive multi-gigapixel stitching datasets (*orthomosaics*), and strict onboard compute ceilings when tracking landing targets in real time.

Working alongside *GNC*, perception supplies the visual velocity, marker pose, and 3D map measurements that let a drone navigate precisely even when GNSS is unreliable or denied.

### Aerial Vision & Mapping Scope
- **2D-to-3D Reconstruction & Orthomosaics:** Camera calibration, feature extraction/matching, epipolar geometry, Structure-from-Motion (`SfM`), bundle adjustment, and georeferenced large-scale orthomosaic generation from UAV surveys.
- **Motion & Dynamic Scene Cues:** Computing dense/sparse **optical flow** (for velocity estimation and obstacle avoidance) and **background estimation / moving-target segmentation** from a moving aerial camera.
- **Fiducial Tracking & UAV SLAM:** Real-time detection of fiducial markers (`ArUco` / `AprilTag`) for autonomous precision landing, and **Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM)** pipelines under aerial motion.

### What You Actually Do in the Lab
Expect progressive coding lab exercises processing real UAV flight imagery into 3D maps and tracking outputs, plus a group perception project compiled into a portfolio/report that drives the questioning in your **individual oral exam**.
