## Overview of Elective & Mobility Slots

Across the 120 ECTS DAS curriculum, you have **20 ECTS of built-in flexibility**: **5 ECTS** in Semester 2 and **15 ECTS** in Semester 3 *(plus an extra 5 ECTS if substituting 15 ECTS Innovative Robotic Technologies with 10 ECTS Project in Robotics)*.

How you spend Semester 3 shapes your thesis and job entry in Denmark:

1. **In-Company Project (`15 ECTS`):** Spend half the Fall semester embedded directly inside a Danish or international robotics/drone company on an applied engineering project. *(Important trade-off: choosing the 15 ECTS In-Company Project uses the elective credits otherwise available to expand your thesis from 30 to 40 ECTS.)*
2. **Study Abroad (`15–30 ECTS`):** Take an exchange semester at an approved partner university (requires Study Board approval).
3. **Experimental Thesis Head-Start (`10 ECTS`) + `5 ECTS` Elective:** Dedicate 10 ECTS in Fall to experimental hardware/flight preparation, turning Semester 4 into a continuous **40 ECTS Master's Thesis**.
4. **Curated Technical Electives (`5–10 ECTS` each):** Pick specialized robotics/software modules at SDU Odense below.
