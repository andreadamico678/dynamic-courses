## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Bio-Inspired Autonomous Systems** (**5 ECTS**, Semester 2) asks how insects, birds, and animals achieve agile flight, obstacle avoidance, and long-range homing using tiny sensory budgets and lightweight compute—without megawatt GPUs or heavy LiDAR stacks.

Building directly on *Introduction to Embodied AI* (Semester 1), you study computational robotic models of **biological perception** (such as optic-flow landing in honeybees, acoustic/echolocation cues, polarization compasses, and neural reflex circuits) and implement them in autonomous systems tested in group experiments.

### Biological Sensing & Autonomy Scope
- **Foundations of Bio-Inspiration & Embodied AI:** Sensor-morphology co-design, reactive sensory-motor loops, and minimal-compute behavioral primitives.
- **Robotic Models of Biological Perception:** Retinal/elementary motion detectors, insect-inspired optic flow divergence control for smooth touchdown, and landmark navigation.
- **Non-Visual Perception Modalities:** Acoustic/ultrasonic sensing, magnetic/celestial cues, and compliant tactile/whisker sensing for cluttered or visually degraded flight.

### What You Actually Do
Teams build and evaluate a bio-inspired perception or control model in simulation or physical robotics experiments, document hypotheses and performance in a **group project report**, and defend both biological principles and robotic implementation individually at the oral exam.
