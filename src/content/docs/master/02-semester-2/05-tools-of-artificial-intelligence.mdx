## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Tools of Artificial Intelligence** (**5 ECTS**, Semester 2) supplies the learning-based half of modern autonomy—covering **neural networks**, **evolutionary/genetic optimization**, and **reinforcement learning (RL)** alongside the gritty experimental challenges of getting ML to actually generalize in engineering systems.

Notice the deliberate split across the year: *Introduction to Embodied AI* (Semester 1) gave you state-space planning and embodied agent loops; *Tools of AI* gives you statistical/neural/RL function approximators and rigorous experimental validation (overfitting diagnostics, hyperparameter sensitivity, reward-shaping pitfalls) so you don't fool yourself with brittle models in Semester 3 or Thesis projects.

### Learning & Optimization Scope
- **Neural Networks & Representation Techniques:** Multi-layer architectures, gradient-based optimization, regularization, and feature/state representations suitable for sensor/control data.
- **Genetic & Evolutionary Algorithms:** Population-based black-box search for morphologies, controller gains, or non-differentiable robotics objectives.
- **Reinforcement Learning (`RL`):** MDPs, value/policy methods, exploration vs. exploitation, and training agents inside simulated control tasks.
- **Experimental ML Methodology:** Train/validation/test leakage prevention, cross-validation, seed variance, and statistical reporting of learning curves.

### What You Actually Do
You implement, tune, and systematically benchmark ML/RL algorithms on engineering tasks—paying close attention to ablation studies and experimental methodology—and deliver a thorough **individual written project report** at the end of the semester.
