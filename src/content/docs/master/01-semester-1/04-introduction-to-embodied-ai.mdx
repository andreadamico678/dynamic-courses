## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Introduction to Embodied Artificial Intelligence** (**5 ECTS**, Semester 1) challenges the pure software view of AI (static datasets in, predictions out) and reframes intelligence through a physical body acting inside an uncertain environment. For an autonomous drone, "thinking" is inseparable from **morphology, noisy sensors, energy budgets, and continuous closed-loop interaction** with physics.

Rather than jumping straight to deep neural nets (covered later in *Tools of AI* and perception courses), this course establishes the **behavior-based, representational, and deliberative planning foundations** that tell an autonomous agent *what sequence of actions achieves a goal safely* in real time.

### Embodied & Planning Scope
- **Embodied & Behavior-Based Architectures:** Reactive vs. deliberative control loops, subsumption/behavior arbitration, and real-time physical constraints.
- **State-Space Representations & Search:** Formulating world models, discrete/continuous transition states, heuristic search, and decision under uncertainty.
- **Autonomous Action Planning:** Goal-directed task planning and integrating perception outputs with low-level execution modules.

### What You Actually Do in the Lab
Teams implement and benchmark an embodied decision/planning agent in simulated or physical robotic testbeds, analyze failure modes in a structured **group report**, and defend both the algorithmic foundations and implementation trade-offs in an **individual oral exam**.
