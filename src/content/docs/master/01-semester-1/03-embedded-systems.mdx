## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Embedded Systems** (**5 ECTS**, Semester 1) sits at the exact boundary between digital hardware and onboard robotics software. Drones carry tight Size, Weight, and Power (SWaP) limits: high-bandwidth sensors (stereo cameras, IMUs, LiDAR, mmWave radar) need deterministic interfaces and fast preprocessing without burning through battery life.

SDU's robotics hardware groups are particularly strong in **FPGA / SoC co-design** (for example, SDU's *MPSoC4Drones* stack pairing Ubuntu + ROS 2 + PX4 with hardware accelerators on programmable logic). This course equips you to write both the low-level hardware/bus interfaces and the C/C++ / ROS 2 drivers above them.

### Hardware–Software Co-Design Scope
- **Digital Systems & Programmable Logic:** Synthesizing sequential/combinational hardware modules in HDL alongside soft or hard processor cores.
- **Peripheral Buses & Memory Sharing:** Deterministic timing and protocol design over `UART`, `SPI`, `I2C`, memory-mapped I/O, and shared memory buffers between logic fabric and CPU.
- **Real-Time Robotics Integration:** Meeting deterministic loop deadlines and offloading compute-heavy tasks (Edge AI / perception kernels) while interfacing cleanly with `ROS 2`.

### What You Actually Do in the Lab
Expect individual starter assignments on digital design and C/peripheral drivers, escalating into a **team hardware-software lab project** on physical boards—building, timing, and verifying a complete embedded robotic subsystem under real latency and resource budgets.
