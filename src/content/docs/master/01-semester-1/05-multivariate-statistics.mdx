## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Multivariate Statistics** (**5 ECTS**, Semester 1) gives you the mathematical machinery behind multi-sensor state estimation, perception benchmarks, and rigorous experimental claims. Real drone pipelines almost never handle a single scalar variable: an IMU outputs 6–9 correlated axes, a Kalman state vector tracks position, velocity, orientation, and biases together via a **covariance matrix $\Sigma$**, and perception models operate over high-dimensional feature vectors.

Together with *Scientific Method*, this course stops you from misreading noisy flight-test plots and equips you with the exact Gaussian/covariance language needed in Semester 2's *GNC* and *Drone Perception*.

### Statistical & Matrix Foundations
- **Random Vectors & Covariance Structure:** Joint distributions, mean vectors, covariance/correlation matrices, linear transformations of Gaussian vectors, and quadratic forms.
- **Estimation & Inference:** Maximum likelihood estimation, confidence regions (ellipsoids), and multivariate hypothesis testing (`Hotelling's T²`, `MANOVA`).
- **Dimensionality & Regression:** Principal Component Analysis (`PCA`), multivariate linear modeling, and variance decomposition on experimental datasets.

### What You Actually Do
You work through quantitative problem sets analyzing multi-dimensional engineering data (typically in MATLAB) and explain the statistical assumptions, matrix derivations, and dataset interpretations directly during your **individual oral exam**.

### Bachelor Bridges
- Vector/matrix algebra and eigendecomposition concepts from **[Linear Systems Theory](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/01-state-models/02-coordinate-basis-similarity-transformations/)** carry directly over to covariance diagonalization and PCA.
