## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Scientific Method** (**5 ECTS**, Semester 1) teaches you how to design experiments, critique engineering papers, and write defensible research reports before you dive into heavy Semester 2–4 projects and your Master's thesis. At the Master's level, showing a video of a drone hovering once is not evidence—you need controlled baselines, clear hypotheses, ablation studies, and awareness of confounding variables and statistical vs. practical significance.

Notably, unlike most other Semester 1 courses that end in oral defenses, *Scientific Method* evaluates you through continuous written portfolio work combined with an end-of-semester written/MCQ test.

### Methodology & Research Craft
- **Epistemology & Engineering Inquiry:** Deductive vs. inductive reasoning, falsifiability, modeling assumptions, and research question formulation.
- **Experimental Design & Reproducibility:** Controlling confounding factors, internal vs. external validity, repeatability in robotics/field trials, and responsible data handling.
- **Critical Analysis & Writing:** Structuring academic engineering reports, dissecting related work, and identifying flawed methodology or cherry-picked benchmarks.

### Assessment Breakdown
Your grade splits evenly between a **semester portfolio** of structured analytical assignments and a **written end-of-term exam** combining multiple-choice questions with short clear-text justifications.
