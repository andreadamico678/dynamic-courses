## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Guidance, Navigation, and Control (`GNC`)** (`T550012101`, **5 ECTS**, Semester 2) is the flagship control core of the Drones & Autonomous Systems specialization. Everything from *Control of Autonomous Systems* (Semester 1) and *Introduction to Drone Technology* converges here into the classic three-part aerospace loop:

1. **Guidance** decides *where the drone should go*—generating collision-free reference paths and smooth time-parameterized trajectories ($\mathbf{p}_{\text{ref}}(t), \mathbf{v}_{\text{ref}}(t), \psi_{\text{ref}}(t)$).
2. **Navigation** estimates *where the drone actually is*—fusing IMU kinematics, GNSS, barometer, or visual measurements into full 6-DOF pose/velocity states despite noise and drift.
3. **Control** computes *motor thrusts and attitude commands*—driving actuators accurately to track guidance references under wind gusts and coupled nonlinear quadrotor dynamics.

### From Equations to Real Autopilot Flight
Unlike pure paper-control courses, SDU's GNC project forces you through the full verification chain:
1. **Mathematical Formalization:** Derive coordinate frames (`NED` vs. `ENU`), rigid-body 6-DOF UAV dynamics, and prove estimator/controller equations cleanly.
2. **Simulation-In-The-Loop (`SITL`):** Run your GNC nodes against **Gazebo + PX4 SITL** (matching the exact `Micro XRCE-DDS` / `MAVLink` topics used on hardware) to verify trajectory tracking and failsafe edge cases safely.
3. **Live Autopilot Implementation:** Deploy your validated algorithms onto an actual **Pixhawk/PX4** hardware testbed or indoor flight platform, log real telemetry, and inspect tracking errors vs. simulation.
