## Course Preface & Mental Model

**Engineering of Innovative Robotic Technologies** (**15 ECTS**, Semester 3) takes up half of your third semester and intentionally pulls you out of narrow single-subject labs into **building a real-world robotic or drone product from scratch**. Working in a multidisciplinary team, you operate almost like an early-stage robotics startup: scoping an industrial or societal problem, engineering a physical/software prototype, **quantitatively measuring usability, quality, and technical viability**, and matching the hardware against a realistic business case and regulatory path.

This is where courses from Semesters 1 and 2 stop being separate subjects—your team might fuse a CAD/3D-printed airframe (*Mechanical Aerial Systems*), Pixhawk/ROS 2 autonomy (*GNC*), onboard cameras (*Drone Perception*), and custom embedded hardware (*Embedded Systems*) into one live demonstrator (for example, autonomous inspection, AED medical delivery, precision agriculture, or counter-UAS).

### Three Pillars of the 15-ECTS Deliverable
1. **Working Robotic/Drone Prototype:** Functional hardware/software demonstrator addressing a concrete use case rather than pure toy slides.
2. **Experimental Validation:** Rigorous user/field/bench measurements evaluating usability, reliability, safety, and technical performance margins.
3. **Entrepreneurship & Pitch:** Value proposition, customer/market feasibility, cost structure, and deployment barriers evaluated alongside the technical architecture.

During the final exam, the external examiner probes **both** your individual technical ownership inside the group prototype and why your engineering choices make sense commercially and operationally.
