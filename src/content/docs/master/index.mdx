## Program Overview & How It Differs From a Bachelor's

This **Master layer** hosts **14 standalone course containers** (currently preface-only course shells, ready to grow into full syllabi independently) for the **MSc in Engineering in Robot Systems — Drones and Autonomous Systems (DAS)** at the **University of Southern Denmark (SDU), Odense**, hosted by the Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute and the SDU UAS Centre.

Unlike an Italian Bachelor's curriculum centered on individual closed-book written algebra exams, SDU runs on **Problem-Based Learning (PBL)**:

- **Team Build + Individual Oral Defense:** In nearly every technical course, teams of 3–4 build and validate a working simulation or physical hardware pipeline, submit mandatory lab/project reports by strict semester deadlines, and then face an **individual oral exam** (Danish **7-point scale**: `-3, 00, 02, 4, 7, 10, 12`, pass $\ge 02$) defending both the team's design decisions and the underlying theory.
- **No Waiting Until Exam Week:** Missing mandatory lab report deadlines usually disqualifies you from sitting the oral exam—work is paced continuously across the semester.
- **Standalone Course Shells:** Opening any course card below takes you into its own isolated course workspace with an independent sidebar, expected hardware/software deliverables, exam format, and links back to your UniCT Bachelor textbooks.

<Divider label="Labs & Engineering Stack" />

To feel comfortable from Week 1 in Odense, focus less on memorizing isolated theorems and more on how **hardware, middleware, estimation, and flight control connect end-to-end**:

1. **Autopilot & Flight Stack:** **PX4 Autopilot** on **Pixhawk** controllers handling inner attitude/rate loops, actuator mixing, safety failsafes, and telemetry (**MAVLink** / **QGroundControl**).
2. **Companion Middleware:** **ROS / ROS 2** running on **Linux** companion computers (**Raspberry Pi**, **NVIDIA Jetson**, or **MPSoC FPGA** boards) communicating with PX4 over **Micro XRCE-DDS / micro-ROS** or **MAVROS**.
3. **Simulation-First Gate:** **Gazebo + PX4 SITL** (Software-In-The-Loop) and **MATLAB/Simulink**—every GNC and perception algorithm proves itself safely in simulation before physical flight.
4. **Physical Prototyping & Flight Arenas:** Indoor **OptiTrack** motion-capture cages (micro-quadrotors / Crazyflie swarms), CAD + 3D printing / composite workshops, and outdoor flight ranges at **UAS Denmark Test Center (HCA Airport)**.

<Divider label="Bachelor Prerequisites Map" />

- **[Linear Systems Theory](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/)** directly underpins *Control of Autonomous Systems* and *Guidance, Navigation and Control* (state-space representation, Lyapunov/eigenvalue stability, observers, loop shaping).
- **[Computer Architecture](/dynamic-courses/computer-architecture/)** directly underpins *Embedded Systems* and *Embedded Linux* (digital FSMs, hardware-software interfaces, memory hierarchies, low-level C execution).
- **[Elettronica](/dynamic-courses/elettronica/)** supports sensor interfacing, power electronics, analog conditioning, and digital CMOS logic across drone hardware labs.
