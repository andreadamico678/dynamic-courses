## Course Preface & Mental Model

The **Master's Thesis** (**30 ECTS** standard full-time in Semester 4, or **40 ECTS** experimental starting with 10 ECTS in Semester 3) is the culmination of the MSc Robot Systems degree. Rather than a purely theoretical literature survey, SDU Robotics and the **SDU UAS Centre (Drone Center)** treat the thesis as an engineering research contribution grounded in simulation, custom hardware/software integration, and experimental validation.

Depending on your career goal, you can execute your thesis directly inside one of SDU's drone research groups (a strong launchpad toward R&D roles or a PhD—SDU also supports 45 ECTS thesis tracks for 4+4 PhD students) or as an **industry-hosted thesis** with Danish drone/robotics companies in the Odense robotics cluster (*Odense Robotics*).

### Facilities & Active Research Directions at SDU UAS Centre
- **Test Facilities:** Indoor **OptiTrack** motion-capture flight arenas (including micro-quadcopter swarms such as **Crazyflie**), electronics/composite workshops, and outdoor BVLOS/test ranges at **UAS Denmark Test Center (HCA Airport, Odense)**.
- **Representative SDU Thesis Topics:**
  - **Autonomous Infrastructure Inspection:** Power-line tracking and wind-turbine inspection combining mmWave radar, computer vision, and onboard ROS 2 perception.
  - **Aerial Manipulation:** Unmanned aerial vehicles equipped with lightweight robotic arms or compliant grippers for physical contact tasks.
  - **Hardware-Accelerated Onboard Autonomy:** Power-efficient deep vision/control running on custom **MPSoC / FPGA + Ubuntu + ROS 2 + PX4** (*MPSoC4Drones*-style co-design).
  - **Heterogeneous Swarms & Counter-UAS (`C-UAS`):** Multi-agent coordination, autonomous **AED emergency delivery**, and wildlife/environmental conservation monitoring.

### Final Assessment
- **Deliverables:** Formal written thesis monograph adhering to *Scientific Method* experimental standards + reproducible code/hardware/simulation artifacts.
- **Exam:** **Individual oral defense** with an external examiner (*censor*) on the 7-point Danish scale.
