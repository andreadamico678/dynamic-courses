## Introduction

Before optimizing tracking speed or control energy, control engineering enforces **stability**: bounded perturbations and bounded actuator commands must never produce unbounded internal growth or actuator saturation.

Part IV connects internal and external stability criteria systematically. Chapters 11–16 locate stationary equilibria $\mathbf{x}_e$, classify internal stability via the eigenvalues and multiplicities of $\mathbf{A}$, evaluate high-degree and symbolic characteristic polynomials without root factoring via the **Routh-Hurwitz array** (including singular rows), compute stabilizing gain bounds $(K_{\min}, K_{\max})$, and contrast internal stability against external **BIBO stability**.

### Running Example

Across Chapters 11–16, we track a third-order parameterized feedback system driven by constant setpoint $u_0$ and proportional loop gain $K$:

$$p(s; K) = s^3 + 6s^2 + 11s + (6 + K) = 0$$

Chapters 11–12 establish hover/trim equilibria and spectral perturbation behavior; Chapters 13–15 build Routh tables (including zero pivots and auxiliary rows) to solve analytically for $K_{\max}$ and sustained oscillation frequency $\omega_{\text{osc}}$; and Chapter 16 links $\int_0^\infty |w(t)|\,dt < \infty$ to hidden unstable modes.

### Real-World Applications

- **Trim Equilibria:** Linearizing hover or cruise trim $(\mathbf{x}_e, \mathbf{u}_0)$ via perturbation coordinates $\mathbf{z} = \mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_e$ brings physical vehicles under LTI stability theorems.
- **Gain Margin Synthesis:** Routh arrays predict exact controller instability thresholds $K_{\max}$ and oscillation frequencies analytically prior to hardware testing.

## Chapter Roadmap

- [11. Equilibrium States](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/04-stability-routh/01-linear-time-invariant-equilibrium-points/) — Solve $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_e + \mathbf{B}\mathbf{u}_0 = \mathbf{0}$ via Rouché-Capelli and shift coordinates to the origin.
- [12. Lyapunov Internal Stability](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/04-stability-routh/02-lyapunov-spectral-internal-stability/) — Classify asymptotic, simple, and unstable modes using eigenvalue real parts and multiplicities ($m_a$ vs. $m_g$).
- [13. Routh-Hurwitz Criterion](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/04-stability-routh/03-routh-hurwitz-tabular-array-construction/) — Count RHP roots from polynomial coefficients using the Routh tabular array.
- [14. Singular Routh Tables](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/04-stability-routh/04-singular-routh-table-zero-elements/) — Resolve isolated zero pivots ($\varepsilon$-method) and all-zero rows via the auxiliary polynomial $A(s)$.
- [15. Parametric Stability Bounds](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/04-stability-routh/05-parametric-feedback-gain-stability-bounds/) — Solve for stabilizing gain intervals $(K_{\min}, K_{\max})$ and boundary oscillation frequencies.
- [16. BIBO Stability](/dynamic-courses/systems-theory/04-stability-routh/06-bounded-input-bounded-output-stability/) — Relate $\lVert w \rVert_1 < \infty$, transfer poles, and hidden pole-zero cancellations to internal stability.
