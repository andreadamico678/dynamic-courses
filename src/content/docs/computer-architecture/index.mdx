## Course Scope & Exam Thresholds

This textbook covers **Computer Architecture** for the Computer Engineering Bachelor's degree (UniCT L-8), pairing **step-by-step mechanical execution** for written/lab exam problems with **concise, practical hardware intuition** for embedded systems and robotics engineering.

The single **written and lab examination** ($2\text{h } 30\text{m}$, $32$ points max, **no oral exam**) enforces **mandatory per-section pass thresholds**—falling short in any single section results in automatic failure regardless of total points:

| Exam Section | Questions | Max Score | Mandatory Minimum | Core Topics |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **I. Digital Systems** | **Q1 + Q2** | $11\text{ pts}$ | **$\ge 6 / 11$** | 2-bit Mealy/Moore sequence detectors, Implication Table reduction, Flip-Flop SOP synthesis |
| **II. Architecture & Performance** | **Q3 + Q4 + Q5** | $12\text{ pts}$ | **$\ge 7 / 12$** | Pipeline/Cache CPI, Amdahl's Law, Cache Tag/Index/Offset & AMAT, Forwarding & Write Policies |
| **III. RISC-V Assembly** | **Q6** | $9\text{ pts}$ | **$\ge 5 / 9$** | C-to-RISC-V (RV32IM) translation with `ecall` I/O, stack frames, and recursion |

## Prerequisites

1. **Boolean Logic:** Basic logic gates (`AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, `XOR`), two's complement signed integers, and hexadecimal notation.
2. **C Programming:** Pointers, arrays, null-terminated strings, stack vs. static memory, and structured loops/recursion.
3. **Discrete Mathematics:** Powers of two ($2^k$), base-2 logarithms ($\log_2$), and modulo arithmetic ($j \bmod N$).

## Engineering Applications

Every abstraction in this course maps directly onto embedded and robotic hardware:

- **Pipelined Datapaths:** On embedded RISC-V and ARM Cortex-M flight controllers, understanding Load-Use stalls, forwarding paths, and compiler instruction scheduling prevents timing jitter in high-rate ($1\,\text{kHz}$) control loops.
- **Cache Hierarchies:** Camera frames and LiDAR buffers streaming via DMA require cache block alignment, Write-Through vs. Write-Back dirty-line management, and conflict-miss avoidance.
- **RISC-V ABI:** Interrupt handlers, system calls, and RTOS context switchers rely directly on stack-pointer (`sp`) discipline and caller/callee-saved register conventions (`ra`, `t0`–`t6`, `s0`–`s11`).
