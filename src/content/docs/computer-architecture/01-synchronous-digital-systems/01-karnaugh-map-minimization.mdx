## Introduction

Implementing a Boolean truth table row by row builds one multi-input `AND` gate per active minterm feeding a wide `OR` gate, wasting silicon area and increasing propagation delay. **Karnaugh map (K-map) minimization** arranges truth-table cells along reflected **Gray-code** axes (`00, 01, 11, 10`) so geometrically adjacent cells differ in exactly one bit, allowing power-of-two blocks of `1`s and **don't-care** wildcards (`X`) to merge visually into a minimal **Sum-of-Products (SOP)** expression.

<Divider label="1 · Example" />

### Dual-Layer Five-Variable Minimization

Consider the 5-variable switching function from a UniCT examination paper, specified by its active minterms $m(\cdot)$ and don't-care conditions $d(\cdot)$:

$$f(a, b, c, d, e) = \sum m(4, 6, 12, 14, 15, 20) + d(7, 13, 22, 28, 29)$$

Because $2^5 = 32$, we split the input space across the most significant bit $a$ into two stacked $4 \times 4$ Gray-coded layers over row bits $bc$ and column bits $de$: **Layer 0 ($a = 0$)** holds indices $0 \dots 15$, while **Layer 1 ($a = 1$)** holds indices $16 \dots 31$ at matching coordinates $(bc, de)$ shifted by $+16$.

**Layer 0 ($a = 0$, minterms $0 \dots 15$):**

| $bc \backslash de$ | $00$ | $01$ | $11$ | $10$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$00$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| **$01$** | **$1$** ($m_4$) | $0$ | **$X$** ($d_7$) | **$1$** ($m_6$) |
| **$11$** | **$1$** ($m_{12}$) | **$X$** ($d_{13}$) | **$1$** ($m_{15}$) | **$1$** ($m_{14}$) |
| **$10$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |

**Layer 1 ($a = 1$, minterms $16 \dots 31$):**

| $bc \backslash de$ | $00$ | $01$ | $11$ | $10$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$00$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| **$01$** | **$1$** ($m_{20}$) | $0$ | $0$ | **$X$** ($d_{22}$) |
| **$11$** | **$X$** ($d_{28}$) | **$X$** ($d_{29}$) | $0$ | $0$ |
| **$10$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |

Next, we expand every `1` into maximal power-of-two rectangles across planar, wrap-around, and inter-layer (3D) adjacencies:

1. **Inter-Layer 3D Group $P_1 = \{m_4, m_{12}, m_{20}, d_{28}\}$:** Column $de = 00$ at rows $bc \in \{01, 11\}$ holds $\{1, 1\}$ on Layer 0 and $\{1, X\}$ at the exact same coordinates on Layer 1. Merging all 4 cells across both layers eliminates $a$ ($0 \to 1$) and $b$ ($0 \to 1$), leaving $c = 1, d = 0, e = 0 \implies c\overline{d}\overline{e}$. Covers active minterms $\{4, 12, 20\}$.
2. **Planar Group $P_2 = \{m_6, d_7, m_{14}, m_{15}\}$:** On Layer 0 ($a = 0$), rows $bc \in \{01, 11\}$ and columns $de \in \{11, 10\}$ form a $2 \times 2$ square. Here $a = 0$, $c = 1$, and $d = 1$ remain constant while $b$ and $e$ vary $\implies \overline{a}cd$. Covers active minterms $\{6, 14, 15\}$.
3. **Alternate 4-Cell Candidates:** Row $bc = 11$ on Layer 0 gives $P_3 = \{m_{12}, d_{13}, m_{15}, m_{14}\} = \overline{a}bc$ (covering $\{12, 14, 15\}$), while outer columns $de \in \{00, 10\}$ at row $bc = 01$ across both layers give $P_4 = \{m_4, m_6, m_{20}, d_{22}\} = \overline{b}c\overline{e}$ (covering $\{4, 6, 20\}$).

Selecting $P_1$ and $P_2$ covers all six `1`-minterms ($\{4, 12, 20\} \cup \{6, 14, 15\} = \{4, 6, 12, 14, 15, 20\}$) with zero uncovered `1`s, yielding a minimal 2-term, 6-literal SOP expression (note that $P_4 + P_3 = \overline{b}c\overline{e} + \overline{a}bc$ is an equally minimal dual cover):

$$f(a, b, c, d, e) = c\overline{d}\overline{e} + \overline{a}cd$$

### Gray-Code Adjacency

Boolean minimization relies on the **Adjacency Law**:

$$A \cdot B + A \cdot \overline{B} = A(B + \overline{B}) = A \cdot 1 = A$$

Whenever two minterms match in every variable except one complemented bit (Hamming distance $d_H = 1$), that changing variable cancels out. A Karnaugh map enforces $d_H = 1$ between every pair of horizontally or vertically neighboring cells by ordering its 2-bit axes in **reflected Gray code**:

$$00 \longrightarrow 01 \longrightarrow 11 \longrightarrow 10$$

Because `10` and `00` also differ in only one bit, opposite edges wrap around like a torus: the top row (`00`) is adjacent to the bottom row (`10`), the leftmost column (`00`) is adjacent to the rightmost column (`10`), and the four outer corner cells form a valid $2 \times 2$ group of four.

For a 4-variable function $f(a, b, c, d)$, decimal minterms $m_0 \dots m_{15}$ map onto $(ab, cd)$ as follows—note how $m_2, m_3$ and rows `11, 10` swap relative to binary counting:

| $ab \backslash cd$ | $00$ | $01$ | $11$ | $10$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$00$** | $m_0$ | $m_1$ | $m_3$ | $m_2$ |
| **$01$** | $m_4$ | $m_5$ | $m_7$ | $m_6$ |
| **$11$** | $m_{12}$ | $m_{13}$ | $m_{15}$ | $m_{14}$ |
| **$10$** | $m_8$ | $m_9$ | $m_{11}$ | $m_{10}$ |

### Implicants and Essential Cover

- **Implicant:** Any rectangular block of `1`s (and optional `X` cells) whose row, column, and layer dimensions are powers of two, giving total cell count $2^k \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32\}$. Grouping $2^k$ cells eliminates $k$ changing variables.
- **Prime Implicant (PI):** A maximally expanded implicant that cannot be doubled to $2^{k+1}$ without absorbing a `0`.
- **Essential Prime Implicant (EPI):** A prime implicant that covers at least one `1`-minterm covered by **no other** prime implicant. Every EPI must appear in the final minimal SOP expression.

### Five-Variable Superposition

For $f(a, b, c, d, e)$, splitting on $a$ stacks two $4 \times 4$ maps over $(bc, de)$:
- **Layer 0 ($a = 0$):** Minterms $m_0 \dots m_{15}$.
- **Layer 1 ($a = 1$):** Minterms $m_{16} \dots m_{31}$, where cell $(bc, de)$ holds index $m_k + 16$.

In addition to planar and wrap-around grouping inside a single layer, any cell at $(bc, de)$ in Layer 0 is adjacent to the **identical $(bc, de)$ cell in Layer 1**. Whenever a group of size $2^k$ on Layer 0 aligns with a matching block of `1`s or `X`s at the exact same coordinates on Layer 1, merge them into one 3D group of size $2^{k+1}$ and drop the literal $a$.

### Step-by-Step Minimization Procedure

1. **Populate Dual-Layer Grids:** Label rows $bc$ and columns $de$ as `00, 01, 11, 10` on both layers. Enter `1` for each index in $M$, `X` for each index in $D$, and `0` elsewhere.
2. **Find Maximal Prime Implicants:** Expand every `1` into the largest possible power-of-two rectangle ($16, 8, 4, 2, 1$) across planar, toroidal wrap-around, and 3D inter-layer dimensions. Include `X` cells **only** when they double a group that contains at least one `1`; never form a group of pure `X`s.
3. **Commit Essential Prime Implicants:** Inspect each `1`-cell individually. If a `1` belongs to only one prime implicant, circle that group as an EPI, add it to the SOP output, and check off all `1`s it covers.
4. **Select Minimum Residual Cover:** If any `1`-cells remain unchecked, cover them using the fewest remaining prime implicants, preferring larger groups (fewer literals). Discard redundant groups whose `1`s are already covered.
5. **Read Invariant Literals:** For each chosen group, inspect $a, b, c, d, e$: keep $x$ if fixed at `1`, keep $\overline{x}$ if fixed at `0`, and omit $x$ if it takes both `0` and `1` across the group. Sum (`+`) the resulting product terms.

<Callout type="tip" title="Practical Hardware Perspective">

On small FPGA/CPLD peripheral blocks—such as custom PWM motor-channel decoders or quadrature optical-encoder glitch filters on drone flight controllers—minimizing SOP literal fan-in directly shortens combinational propagation delay $t_{\text{comb}}$. In large ASIC flows, synthesis tools (Yosys, Synopsys Design Compiler) replace manual K-maps with multi-level heuristic minimizers (such as Espresso) mapped onto standard `NAND`/`NOR`/`AOI` cell libraries.

</Callout>

### Exam Execution Checklist

1. Write Gray headers `00, 01, 11, 10` on **both** axes before plotting a single minterm.
2. Map indices $0 \dots 15$ to Layer 0 ($a = 0$) and subtract $16$ to place indices $16 \dots 31$ on Layer 1 ($a = 1$).
3. Check 3D inter-layer alignment and 4-corner wrap-around on every candidate group before declaring it prime.
4. Circle EPIs via uniquely covered `1`s first, then cover only the remaining uncovered `1`s.
5. Verify that every `X` cell outside your selected groups is simply ignored (left at `0`).

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistakes">

1. **Row or Column Swap (`10` Before `11`):** Labeling axes `00, 01, 10, 11` places $d_H = 2$ neighbors next to each other (`01` vs. `10`), invalidating every implicant crossing the middle boundary.
2. **Non-Power-of-Two Blocks:** Grouping 3 or 6 contiguous cells is algebraically illegal; valid sizes are strictly $1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32$.
3. **Treating Don't-Cares as Mandatory `1`s:** Covering `X` cells that contain no uncovered `1`s injects extra product gates into your circuit.
4. **Missing Inter-Layer Merges:** Leaving two identical groups separate on Layer 0 and Layer 1 produces $\overline{a}P + aP$ instead of factoring out $P$.

</Callout>

1. **Dual-Layer Karnaugh Maps:**

   | **$11$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |
   | **$10$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |

   **Layer 1 ($a = 1$):**

   | $bc \backslash de$ | $00$ | $01$ | $11$ | $10$ |
   | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
   | **$00$** | **$X$** ($d_{16}$) | **$X$** ($d_{17}$) | $0$ ($m_{19}$) | **$X$** ($d_{18}$) |
   | **$01$** | **$1$** ($m_{20}$) | **$1$** ($m_{21}$) | **$X$** ($d_{23}$) | $0$ |
   | **$11$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |
   | **$10$** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |

2. **Maximal Prime Implicants & Essentiality:**
   - **Group 1 ($\{m_0, m_2, d_{16}, d_{18}\}$):** Outer columns $de \in \{00, 10\}$ on row $bc = 00$ wrap around on Layer 0 and align with $\{d_{16}, d_{18}\}$ on Layer 1, forming a 4-cell 3D group. Variables: $a$ cancels, $bc = 00 \implies \overline{b}\overline{c}$, $e = 0 \implies \overline{e}$. Term: $\overline{b}\overline{c}\overline{e}$. Uniquely covers $m_0$ and $m_2$ $\implies$ **EPI**.
   - **Group 2 ($\{m_5, m_7, m_{21}, d_{23}\}$):** Row $bc = 01$, columns $de \in \{01, 11\}$ on Layer 0 align with $\{m_{21}, d_{23}\}$ on Layer 1, forming a 4-cell 3D group. Variables: $a$ cancels, $bc = 01 \implies \overline{b}c$, $e = 1 \implies e$. Term: $\overline{b}ce$. Uniquely covers $m_7$ $\implies$ **EPI**.
   - **Group 3 ($\{d_{16}, d_{17}, m_{20}, m_{21}\}$):** Planar $2 \times 2$ block on Layer 1 ($a = 1$) across rows $bc \in \{00, 01\}$ ($\overline{b}$) and columns $de \in \{00, 01\}$ ($\overline{d}$). Term: $a\overline{b}\overline{d}$. Uniquely covers $m_{20}$ (since $m_{19} = 0$ blocks a 3D merge with $\{d_4, m_5\}$ unless reduced to size 4 $\{d_4, m_5, m_{20}, m_{21}\} = \overline{b}c\overline{d}$, which gives the same 3-literal count; if $\{d_4, m_5, m_{20}, m_{21}\}$ is also noted, either $\overline{b}c\overline{d}$ or $a\overline{b}\overline{d}$ covers $m_{20}$ with 3 literals!).

3. **Minimal SOP Expression:**
   All six minterms $\{0, 2, 5, 7, 20, 21\}$ are covered:

   $$f(a, b, c, d, e) = \overline{b}\overline{c}\overline{e} + \overline{b}ce + a\overline{b}\overline{d}$$

<Exercise client:load id="ex-1-2" title="Exercise 1.2 — Toroidal Wrap-Around and Unused Rows" level="Exam" hint="Check the four corners of the 4x4 map first, then use the don't-care rows 11 and 10 to expand the left block across all four rows.">

A 4-variable combinational output $u(a, b, c, d)$ driven by two state bits $ab$ (where states `10` and `11` are unused don't-cares on columns `00, 01`) has the following Karnaugh map:

| $ab \backslash cd$ | $00$ | $01$ | $11$ | $10$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$00$** | **$1$** | **$1$** | $0$ | **$1$** |
| **$01$** | **$1$** | **$1$** | $0$ | $0$ |
| **$11$** | **$X$** | **$X$** | $0$ | $0$ |
| **$10$** | **$X$** | **$X$** | $0$ | **$1$** |

1. Find the maximal prime implicant covering the `1`s in columns $cd \in \{00, 01\}$.
2. Find the maximal prime implicant covering the remaining `1`s at $(00, 10)$ and $(10, 10)$.
3. Write the minimal SOP expression for $u(a, b, c, d)$ and count the total number of literals.

<details className="dc-solution">
<summary>Reveal Worked Solution</summary>

1. **Eight-Cell Vertical Block:** Treating the four `X` cells at $(11, 00), (11, 01), (10, 00), (10, 01)$ as `1`s merges all 8 cells of columns $cd \in \{00, 01\}$ across every row $ab$. Both $a$ and $b$ cancel completely, $d$ changes ($0 \to 1$), and only $c = 0$ stays constant $\implies \overline{c}$.
2. **Four-Corner Toroidal Block:** The remaining uncovered `1`s sit at $(00, 10)$ and $(10, 10)$. Together with $(00, 00) = 1$ and $(10, 00) = X$, the four outer corners $\{ab \in \{00, 10\}\} \times \{cd \in \{00, 10\}\}$ form a 4-cell wrap-around group where $b = 0$ and $d = 0$ remain constant $\implies \overline{b}\overline{d}$.
3. **Minimal SOP:**

   $$u(a, b, c, d) = \overline{c} + \overline{b}\overline{d}$$

   This uses 2 product terms and $1 + 2 = 3$ total literals.

</details>
